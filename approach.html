Approach / Building in the open

A services layer for credible environmental-market transactions.

Solvura-X combines sourcing, screening, structuring, coordination and brokerage around authorised supply.

In incorporation in Rabat - building in the open

01 / Workflow

The intended operating sequence.

The workflow provides a clear control path from requirements through reconciliation. Each step is intended to have a defined purpose and handoff.

  1. 01

    Define

    Establish the transaction requirements, counterpart roles, information scope and decision criteria.

  2. 02

    Collect (Screen)

    Gather relevant supply and supporting information, then screen it against the defined requirements.

  3. 03

    Check

    Apply structured checks to provenance, documentation, authorisation context and transaction conditions.

  4. 04

    Structure

    Shape the transaction architecture, responsibilities, required records and sequencing before coordination begins.

  5. 05

    Coordinate

    Coordinate counterpart actions, information exchanges and supporting roles through an agreed process.

  6. 06

    Reconcile

    Bring transaction information and records together to support a clear closing trail and consistent record set.

intended operating model, not a claim every component is live

02 / Integrity

Safeguards built into the operating logic.

Provenance

Provenance before presentation

Supply is intended to move through provenance and supporting-documentation checks before it is positioned for transaction consideration.

Independence

Independent roles stay independent

The operating model is designed to preserve defined boundaries between counterpart roles, specialist activities and transaction coordination.

Counting control

Double-counting as a control objective

Record consistency and traceable transaction information are intended to support the control objective of avoiding double-counting.

Visibility

Controls that can be understood

Process steps, information requirements and responsibility boundaries are intended to remain legible to relevant counterparties.
