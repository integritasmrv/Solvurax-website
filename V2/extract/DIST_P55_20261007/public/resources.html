CBAM cost estimator

The EU Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism, CBAM, prices the emissions embedded in imports of cement, iron and steel, aluminium, fertilisers, electricity and hydrogen. Estimate the certificates and their cost for your goods.

Export inputs

Each sector sets the unit, the gases and the emissions counted.

Choose a sector to list its goods.

EU importers above 50 tonnes of CBAM goods need the status of authorised CBAM declarant.

From verified installation data, or the EU default value for the product.

Optional. EU benchmarks are set in Implementing Regulation 2025/2620. Left empty, the estimate takes the benchmark as equal to your emissions per tonne; a lower EU benchmark raises the cost.

The share of emissions charged rises each year as EU free allocation phases out.

Only a price effectively paid, net of any rebate, counts.

Optional. The EU sets it from EU ETS allowance auction prices: a quarterly average in 2026, a weekly average from 2027.
