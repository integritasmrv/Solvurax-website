01 / Operating model

Services shaped around authorised supply.

Solvura-X is developing a services and structuring layer for environmental-market transactions, with an emphasis on provenance, coordination and transaction readiness.

Three service lines: introduction and structuring; advisory and structuring; digital MRV and data services.

Structuring

Transaction structures

Intended to frame transaction requirements, counterpart roles, documentation and control points before authorised supply is presented for market access.

Sourcing

Authorised supply

Intended to source and screen authorised supply against defined criteria before it enters the transaction workflow.

Data

MRV and technical interoperability with third-party registries

Intended to support MRV data handling and technical interoperability with third-party registries through structured information flows and defined control points. Verification and issuance always sit with independent, recognised third parties.

Access

Market access

Intended to connect qualified supply and counterpart requirements through a coordinated introduction and transaction-support process.

02 / Workflow

A disciplined sequence from definition to reconciliation.

The operating model is designed as a controlled progression through six named steps.

  1. 01

    Define

    Set the transaction requirements, roles and information needed to proceed.

  2. 02

    Collect

    Gather relevant supply and supporting information for structured screening.

  3. 03

    Check

    Apply defined checks to provenance, documentation and transaction conditions.

  4. 04

    Structure

    Shape the transaction architecture, responsibilities and required documentation.

  5. 05

    Coordinate

    Coordinate counterpart actions and information through the agreed transaction path.

  6. 06

    Reconcile

    Bring transaction information and records together for a clear closing trail.

03 / Integrity

Controls begin with provenance.

The intended safeguards place provenance before presentation, preserve independence between roles, and treat avoidance of double-counting as a core control objective.

The result is an operating posture designed to make the basis of a transaction legible before it moves through the workflow.

See the safeguards

04 / Pathways

A defined route for each counterpart.

Developers Prepare authorised supply for a structured transaction path. Buyers Clarify requirements and receive transaction-ready supply information. Partners Coordinate specialist roles around a defined transaction workflow. Investors Follow a transparent operating model with visible controls and defined scope.
